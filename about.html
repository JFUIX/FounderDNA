FounderDNA starts with a founder self-assessment that helps people reflect on their strengths, gaps, and next actions. Optional guided briefs then help them describe business obstacles and request practical support. It starts as a small, manually managed service maintained by JFUIX.

Assessment first. Three ways to move forward.

Find Direction brings structure to the situation and the next decision. Find People identifies the capabilities, working relationships, and expertise that could help. Gather Support explores practical resources and carefully scoped introductions.

Your FounderDNA is a private, evolving business profile: purpose, stage, strengths, challenges, team capabilities, and support needs. The separate assessment offers a self-reported reflection score, not a personality diagnosis, ranking of founders, or prediction of success.

What the early service does

You can prepare a guided brief, preview or download it, and sign in to send it for human review. A confirmed reference means the request was saved to the private queue. Return to My Progress for team messages, useful next actions, weekly check-ins, and decisions about proposed introductions.

Expert applications support skills, availability, portfolio links, and private CV/portfolio PDFs. Applications are reviewed; they do not automatically become public listings or guarantee work.

What to expect

Review is manual. There is no guaranteed response deadline, expert availability, suitable connection, or funding outcome. Investment introductions and non-investment support are separate. We do not process payments, run live investment offers, or collect donations here. Any paid service or engagement requires separate, clear terms before you agree.

Contact & support

For your submitted brief, sign in to My Progress and add a private message to the request. Do not put business secrets or personal contact details in public milestones.

For website issues or help accessing your account, contact the project maintainer on GitHub. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Ask for a private contact route without posting passwords, sign-in links, email addresses, or confidential business details.

A place to share progress

Milestones are a supporting space for lessons and progress. Public publication needs explicit consent and team moderation. Your support request and expert documents are not automatically published.
