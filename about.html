A PLACE TO BUILD IN THE OPEN

Small steps.
Better connections.

FounderDNA is an early access community for founders and small businesses, maintained by JFUIX.

What you can do today

  • Create a public business profile and share business milestones.
  • Reply to other founders, edit your own milestones, and check in-app reply notifications.
  • Report content and hide members from your own view.
  • Use the private brief tool to organize your thoughts and download a copy.

Clear expectations

Business names and identities are self-described. Profiles are not verified by FounderDNA. The community does not currently arrange investments, guarantee matches, provide expert consultations, or send private support requests. Reply notifications are shown inside the site; they are not emailed.

Contact & support

For a bug, question, or request for help, contact the project maintainer on GitHub. GitHub issues are public: do not include passwords, sign-in links, email addresses, or confidential business information. A GitHub account is required to open an issue.

For content complaints, use the Report control on the relevant post or reply while signed in. Reports are stored privately for project-owner review. There is no guaranteed response time during early access.

Your first step

You do not need a polished success story. Share a small milestone, what you learned, and a question that another founder could help answer.
