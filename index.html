BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few clear answers.

Who can use FounderDNA?

Founders with a new idea, early-stage entrepreneurs, existing businesses facing obstacles, and people restarting or rethinking a business. Experts and professional service providers can apply to offer their skills.

What happens after I send a brief?

You receive a reference when your submission is saved. The team reviews requests manually and can respond in My Progress. During early access, no response deadline or suitable match is guaranteed.

Will anyone else see my business information?

Briefs and contact details are private to you and authorized reviewers. An external introduction needs your approval for a named recipient and a description of the information to be shared. Public milestones have separate consent and review.

Does it cost anything?

Submitting a brief is free during early access. Any proposed professional service or paid engagement must have its costs and terms agreed separately before you commit. The website does not process payments.

Can you find funding for my business?

We can review your needs and consider suitable resources or introductions. This is not a funding commitment, investment offer, or guarantee. Investment introductions and non-investment support are separate pathways.

How do I offer my expertise?

Choose Offer Your Expertise and describe your skills, engagement interests, and availability. You can attach a CV and portfolio as private PDFs up to 2 MB each, or add a portfolio link. Applying does not automatically create a public expert listing or guarantee work.

Can I start before signing in?

Yes. You can prepare, preview, and download a brief first. Sign-in is needed to submit it securely and return to its updates. Drafts are saved on this browser for seven days, so avoid a shared device or clear your draft when finished.
