This notice describes the early access FounderDNA service maintained by JFUIX. About and contact routes.

Private by default

Business requests contain the situation, strengths, challenges, capabilities, goals, and support needs you choose to share. Your contact name and verified sign-in email are used for follow-up. Submitted briefs and attachments are visible only to you and authorized reviewers, not other users or public business profiles.

We ask for the context needed to review your request, not passwords, identity documents, bank details, or confidential information you are not authorized to share. Business information can still be sensitive: include only what is useful and appropriate.

Previewing, sending, and receipts

You can prepare, preview, or download a brief before sending. Downloading does not submit it. Sending requires sign-in and explicit permission for private storage, review, and request-related follow-up. A returned reference confirms the request was saved. No external introduction or public publication happens automatically.

Expert documents

CV and portfolio uploads are optional PDFs up to 2 MB each. Selected files are uploaded only when you submit with document permission. They use private storage and are made available to reviewers only when attached to a submitted request. Download links expire after a short time. Technical failure may leave an unused private upload, which the project owner can remove.

External introductions

A proposal names the recipient, explains the purpose, and lists the information to be shared. You may approve or decline it in My Progress. Consent applies only to that proposal, not unrestricted sharing. You may withdraw permission for future sharing; information already shared cannot be recalled. The team arranges approved introductions manually.

Public milestones and profiles

A public milestone requires explicit publication consent and human approval. Only the business name, public title/story and chosen public attribution are published. Private brief details, emails, and documents stay separate. A milestone submitted through the guided brief uses the attribution “Business update”; the milestone community form explicitly includes your chosen account display name.

Community business profiles are optional, public, and self-described. They are separate from your private FounderDNA business context. Public content may be copied, indexed, or shared by other people. Changes to a published milestone return it to review before it becomes public again.

Drafts and sessions on your device

Brief and milestone drafts are stored in this browser for seven days so you can return after email sign-in. Expired drafts are removed when next accessed. Signed-in private brief drafts are restored only for the same account in the application. Browser storage is not an encrypted personal vault: avoid shared devices and clear your draft when finished. Selected files are not kept in browser drafts and may need to be reselected.

Your browser also keeps sign-in session tokens. Sign out on shared devices. Supabase provides authentication and stores account data and operational logs under its privacy policy.

Progress updates, reports, and notifications

Request conversations, weekly check-ins, and introduction decisions are private to the request owner and authorized reviewers. Updates are shown in My Progress; no automatic email notifications or weekly reminder emails are sent. Community reply notifications, hidden-member preferences, and reports remain private to their owners and project administrators.

Retention and deletion

Submitted requests and documents remain until the project owner removes them or the associated account is deleted, subject to operational backups and provider retention. Contact the maintainer for a data or account-deletion request. Do not publish personal account details in a GitHub issue; ask for a private contact route.

You can delete your own community posts and replies. Deleting a milestone removes its replies and associated notifications. Copies made outside the service may remain.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase stores account, request, community, and document data. Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve font and client-library resources. These providers receive ordinary network information when your browser requests their services. This version has no advertising or analytics tracker installed by FounderDNA.

Professional community rules

  • Keep posts business focused and accurate. No impersonation, misleading funding claims, or fabricated achievements.
  • No spam, harassment, threats, or sharing private details without permission.
  • State whether a proposed engagement is paid work, mentorship, collaboration, or an investment discussion. Agree on terms separately.
  • Use Report for content that needs human review. Reports are not an emergency reporting service.

We never use your brief as a personality test or promise business success. The purpose is useful understanding, responsible follow-up, and practical progress.
