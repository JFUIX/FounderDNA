Privacy &
community rules.

This notice describes the current early access website maintained by JFUIX. Contact and project details.

Public content

Your public name, business profile, optional website, milestones, and replies can be read by anyone, including people without an account. Avoid confidential information. Business names and identities are self-described. Public pages may be copied, shared, or indexed by others.

Email sign-in & account data

Supabase provides email sign-in and stores account and session information. Your sign-in email is not included in public profiles, posts, or replies. Your browser keeps session tokens so you can stay signed in. Sign out on shared devices. Supabase also processes operational and authentication logs under its own terms and privacy policy.

Private briefs & device drafts

The homepage brief tool does not send your answers to FounderDNA or Supabase. Answers stay in the page until you leave, unless you download a copy. Downloaded files remain on your device.

Community milestone and reply drafts are saved in this browser's local storage so they can survive sign-in and reloads. Drafts expire after 7 days and are removed when the app next accesses them. You can clear the milestone draft using Clear draft, or remove all device data in your browser settings. Drafts are not shared across devices and can be lost if storage is cleared. Publishing a draft makes that post public.

Reports, hidden members & notifications

Reports are visible only to the reporter and project administrators. They are stored in the project database for review. Hidden-member preferences and reply notifications belong to your signed-in account and are not public.

Hiding a member removes their posts and replies from your view. It does not prevent them from seeing public content. New reply notifications are suppressed between hidden members. Notifications appear inside the site and are not emailed.

Editing, removal & retention

You can edit your own milestones and delete your own posts and replies. Deleting a milestone also deletes its replies and related notifications. Published content remains until its owner or the project administrator removes it; reports remain until reviewed or the reported content is removed. Copies made by other people may remain after deletion.

For account deletion or other data questions, use the maintainer contact route to request assistance. Do not place personal account details in a public GitHub issue. Account deletion is handled by the project owner rather than a self-service button.

Hosting & external services

GitHub Pages hosts the site; Supabase stores community and account data. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts and its sign-in library from jsDelivr. These services receive ordinary connection information when your browser requests their resources. Optional business websites are external sites with their own policies. No advertising or analytics tracker is installed by this version of FounderDNA.

Community rules

  • Share accurate progress. Do not impersonate another person or business.
  • Ask useful questions and discuss ideas respectfully. No harassment, hate, threats, or spam.
  • Do not post private contact details, confidential information, or content you do not have permission to share.
  • Describe commercial interests clearly. A public conversation does not establish an investment, employment, or service agreement.
  • Use Report for content that needs review. Moderators may remove content that violates these rules.

FounderDNA is an early access service. The project owner reviews reports manually; this is not an emergency reporting service.
